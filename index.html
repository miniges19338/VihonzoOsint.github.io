01. What is OSINT?

Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) is the practice of collecting and analyzing information from publicly available sources. It is not hacking; it is the art of connecting dots that are already on the internet. Every post, like, comment, and public record leaves a digital footprint. OSINT is about finding those footprints.

02. Core Techniques

> Username Enumeration

People often use the same nickname across multiple platforms. By finding one username, you can map a person's entire digital presence (social media, forums, gaming accounts).

> Email & Phone Lookup

Emails and phone numbers are connected to data breaches, account registrations, and public directories. Checking if an email was pwned in a data breach is step one.

> Image Forensics (Reverse Search)

Finding where an image was taken or if it's fake. Using reverse image search engines and analyzing EXIF metadata (GPS coordinates, camera model) left in the file.

> Network Reconnaissance

Mapping domains, finding subdomains, and checking open ports. This is useful for cybersecurity analysts to find vulnerabilities in their own systems.

04. Advanced Search (Google Dorks)

Google Dorks are search queries that use advanced operators to find hidden information. Examples:

site:*.gov.ua "index of"
intext:"password" filetype:txt
site:vk.com "user" inurl:profile

05. Stay Legal

OSINT is legal because it uses public data. However, using this information to stalk, dox, or harass individuals is a crime. Always respect privacy and follow the law.
